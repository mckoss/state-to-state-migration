Sources and notes

  • Flows: U.S. Census Bureau, State-to-State Migration Flows, American Community Survey 1-year estimates, 2005–2024. No 2020 table was released, because 2020 ACS 1-year data did not meet quality standards. "Current residence" is the destination and "residence 1 year ago" the origin. Estimates carry a 90% margin of error (shown as ±); small flows are often not statistically different from zero. Cells marked N were suppressed by the Census Bureau and are treated as zero on the map.
  • Population estimates: U.S. Census Bureau, Vintage 2025 state population totals and components of change (NST-EST2025-ALLDATA). Each year runs July 1 to June 30; 2020 covers April 1 to June 30, 2020 only. Rates are per 1,000 residents.
  • All data is served from copies stored with this site; nothing is fetched from census.gov at run time. The copies are checked against the Census Bureau monthly and updated when new data is published.
